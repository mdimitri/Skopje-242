Тука стави своја музика (mp3, ogg, wav, flac) – играта ја вчитува целата.
Put your own music here (mp3, ogg, wav, flac; m4a/aac/opus are not supported by the engine).
Every sub-folder becomes a radio station named after the folder; loose files are shared out between a few stations.
In a vehicle: Q / E (or , / .) change station, the last step is radio off.
