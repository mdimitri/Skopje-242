Тука стави своја музика (mp3, ogg, wav, flac) – играта ја вчитува целата.
Put your own music here (mp3, ogg, wav, flac; m4a/aac/opus are not supported by the engine).
Every sub-folder is a radio station (a genre) named after the folder, e.g. music/RAP. Loose files next to the folders share one extra station.
Each station plays its songs in a shuffled order.
In a vehicle: Q / E change the station (the last step is radio off); , / . skip to the previous / next song of the station.
