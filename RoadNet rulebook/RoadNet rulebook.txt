asphalt is always rendered above the ground surface
road markings are always rendered above the asphalt
sidewalks are always raised above the asphalt and people and vehicle climb on this raised surface
medians are always raised 2x the sidewalks above the asphalt and are drivable if approached fast enough
a slip lane always peels off the right most lane of the road and always connects to the right most lane of the joining road
the road after a slip lane peels of must continue with the same left most edge, so the left most lanes remain the same (if their number is reduced)
all widenings or narrowing of roads due to addition or removal of lanes must happen gradually over 25–50 m so that the width doesn't change abruptly, the sidewalks and medians follow this too
traffic islands are always raised above the asphalt the same height as sidewalks and people and vehicles climb on this raised surface
traffic light gantries are always perpendicular to the direction of the road leading into an intersection or crossing
zebra crossings are always perpendicular to the lane(s) and always connect two walkable areas such as sidewalks or islands or footpaths
traffic signs and individual traffic light poles and street lighting poles are never placed over asphalt, they can be placed on sidewalks or medians
traffic never drives over non-asphalt surfaces, unless when trying to solve a jam or smartly avoiding traffic where it can swerve over on a sidewalk but never with more than 70% of its body width
traffic light gantries are put on every road that has two or more lanes, gantries cannot be put closer than 50m from each other if regulating the same traffic lane
